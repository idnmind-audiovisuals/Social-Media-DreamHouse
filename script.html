Dream House · The Trailer

Voice Over & Script

Two parts of one film. The voiceover carries the inner memory of the experience; the script will translate that emotional journey into image, action, rhythm, and sound.

Part 01

Dream House Voice Over

Length2–3 minutes
StyleCinematic art-film trailer
Emotional arc

The film begins like a dream the viewer cannot fully understand. It gradually becomes warmer, more human, and more alive. By the end, the audience realizes that what initially seemed imagined has become real.

Wonder→Arrival→Belonging→Creation→Transformation→Expansion
Voice direction

The voice should feel intimate and reflective—as though someone is remembering the experience, not narrating or explaining it. Leave generous silence between passages. The delivery is vulnerable, curious, and slightly dreamlike.

01

Opening

What is this place?

Silence

I feel like…
I’ve been here before.

Long pause

Or maybe…
I’ve only dreamed about it.

02

Arrival

A place where things feel possible.

Pause

Where I could arrive as I am…
and still express all of me.

03

Connection

At first…
we were strangers.

Long silence

I don’t remember
when that changed.

04

Creation

An idea sounds different…

once it is spoken into a room
full of people who believe in it.

Pause

Maybe dreams were never meant
to live inside one person.

05

Transformation

Somewhere between the work…

the dancing…

the conversations that lasted
longer than the night…

Pause

something began to feel different.

06

Resolution

The things we imagined…

started finding their way
into the world.

Long pause

And suddenly…

we weren’t waiting
to live the dream anymore.

07

Ending

We were already inside it.

Silence

Maybe…
this is the dream.
